# QuietSpace

QuietSpace is a social media platform for people who are tired of the noise, endless scrolling, and social pressure of mainstream apps. Share your thoughts without worrying about likes, followers, or going viral. Every post is meaningful. Choose a mood, write what matters, and connect with people who truly understand.

**Speak softly. Your words matter.**

---

## Features

- **Slow social:** Post as often as you like, but with mindful pacing
- **Mutual connections only:** No one-way followers
- **5 moods to choose from:** Quiet, Cozy, Reflective, Focus, Creative
- **Meaningful reactions:** Coffee, Wind, Book. No like counts to chase
- **Private Journal:** Personal reflections you never have to share
- **Direct Messages:** Open to all users, safe by design
- **Community groups:** Built around shared vibes, post-based, not chat noise
- **Trusted device verification:** Keeps your account secure
- **Personalization:** Custom frames, fonts, and gradients
- **Earn coins:** Watch ads and unlock premium customization
